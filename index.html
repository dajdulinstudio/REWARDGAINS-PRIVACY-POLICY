RewardJoy ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services, compliant with the latest 2026 data protection standards and Google Play requirements.

1. Data Collection and Transparency

We collect information only as necessary to provide our rewards service and prevent fraudulent activities.

A. Information You Provide to Us:

  • Account Data: When you sign in via Google, we collect your email address and basic profile information to create your RewardJoy account.

B. Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device Identifiers: We collect a unique device hash and hardware identifier. Crucial for 2026 compliance: This is used strictly for fraud prevention (preventing multiple account abuse) and is not shared with data brokers.
  • Advertising ID: We use the Google Advertising ID to provide personalized rewards and ad-based features.

2. Mandatory 2026 Data Deletion Policy

How to Request Account and Data Deletion

In accordance with 2026 global privacy standards, we provide clear and easy methods to permanently delete your data:

  1. In-App Deletion (Instant): Open the "Profile" screen, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete Account". This action immediately triggers a batch process that deletes your profile, points, tickets, and history from our database.
  2. Web-Based/Email Request: You can request full data deletion without installing the app by emailing us at dajdulinstudio@gmail.com.

*Upon request, all data is permanently erased from our production servers. Backup logs are purged within 30 days.

3. Third-Party Integration and Disclosure

We use trusted industry-standard partners to power our app features:

  • Google Firebase: For real-time database management and secure authentication.
  • AdMob (Google): To serve advertisements that fund your rewards.
  • Tapjoy: Our primary offerwall provider. They process task completions and point assignments.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA/GDPR Compliance)

Our service is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, it is immediately deleted from our systems.

5. Anti-Fraud and Security

To maintain a fair ecosystem for all users, we use automated systems to detect VPNs, emulators, and script-based cheating. Users found violating our Terms of Service will have their data flagged and potentially removed.
